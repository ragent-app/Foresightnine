# ForesightNine risk disclosure draft

**Status: pending legal, compliance, and product approval. Not a public launch notice.**

ForesightNine is intended to present research scenarios and portfolio simulations for education and exploration. It is not investment advice, a recommendation tailored to a person's circumstances, a prediction, or a promise of returns. Probabilities are model estimates and may be uncalibrated. Historical observations and simulated weak/main/strong paths do not predict future performance.

The FM Raw result is an experimental, client-computed research output. Public Default is a separate, visible safety layer that can alter or remove exposures for long-only, concentration, volatility, liquidity, implementability, data-freshness, proxy, and lot-size reasons. Neither layer is a trade instruction. Amounts are illustrative conversions of user-entered capital using a stated data snapshot, FX snapshot, instrument price, lot size, and method version.

ForesightNine does not connect to a brokerage account, submit or route orders, hold or custody funds or assets, settle trades, provide margin, or perform execution. It does not offer brokerage or custody services. Any future integration would require separate product, security, licensing, regulatory, and legal approval; no such integration is claimed here.

Users remain responsible for independent verification, suitability, taxes, liquidity, fees, execution conditions, currency risk, data errors, model risk, concentration, drawdown, and loss of capital. A stale, invalid, future-dated, historical-only, or provider-unavailable data snapshot may be shown only as clearly labelled historical/simulated research. It must not produce current executable Public Default amounts.

Research evidence can be incomplete, incorrectly graded, unavailable, user-supplied, or impossible to independently verify. A private claim or model inference is not public evidence. Worldline events, reveal dates, causal links, and probability intervals can be revised. No output should be treated as a factual forecast or a guarantee.

Before any public production release, the responsible owners must approve this disclosure for each target jurisdiction, confirm licensed market-data rights, complete investment-advice analysis, publish service terms and privacy notice, and verify abuse, monitoring, incident-response, secret-handling, and data-deletion controls. This draft does not represent that any gate has passed.
