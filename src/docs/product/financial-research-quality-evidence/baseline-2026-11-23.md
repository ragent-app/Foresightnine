# Financial research delivery: frozen baseline and acceptance protocol

Recorded before candidate assessment against commit `aaab3d75dd5bf51da5f0ab4d8de74ec34997fb13`. The supplied implementation plan was untracked at baseline; the replay imports the baseline commit's API package and contract schema, not the mutable worktree.

## Frozen independent replay

Fifteen independently authored fictional task groups, two candidate conclusions per group, 30 cases total. Each pair has the same question and synthetic source; one conclusion is supported, one contradicts or exceeds the source or answers a different measure. Groups include rights, policy timing and scope, currency, network, measurements, causation, conditional terms, personal cash-flow risk, definitions and Chinese. Full source text, questions, claims, expected outcomes and runner are kept outside the worktree until the candidate has been scored; they will be archived with the results after the blind evaluation. They are not used for developer tuning. This is a small hand-authored challenge set, not the approximately 30 independent base tasks proposed in the plan, and is not representative of natural usage.

## Pre-candidate decision rules

No opposite contractual term, proxy substituted for the requested measurement, or unconditional recommendation that disregards an explicit financial deadline may pass in the applicable severe cases. A single failure blocks claiming that severe gate passed. Report every other false acceptance and every false rejection, including their category and denominator, without asserting a population rate or inventing a numerical release threshold. Positive controls must also be reported: rejecting all cases cannot establish delivery quality. Provider calls and repeat paid outbound calls are outside this replay and need separately authorized workflow tests; do not claim their zero-tolerance criterion from this score.

Candidate replay is run once after integration using the same frozen inputs and runner. A later final-release rerun is separately identified, not conflated with the initial candidate. The freeze is retained in evaluator-private temporary storage during development; its checksums above permit integrity verification before results are disclosed. If that storage is lost, this holdout cannot be treated as recoverable blind evidence and must be reported as such.

Full acceptance additionally requires fixed-material native workflow evidence, independent semantic-review comparison with actual incremental cost, natural-question end-to-end testing, and authorized real-source evaluation. This offline gate does not measure feasible-action execution, unnecessary clarification, repeated calls, actual useful-delivery cost, or user-interface behavior. Those remain unverified unless separately recorded.

A separate baseline run of six existing offline workflow tests passed on the archived baseline: lifecycle cancellation and permission revocation, supervisor restart with budget preservation, unknown claimed tool on recovery, denied/FRED source reads, unknown external result, and exclusion of unauthorized source metadata. These are scripted model tests; they verify only the specific assertions in those tests. Baseline pytest emitted one Starlette/httpx deprecation warning. They do not establish private-data minimization in public search or user-wait/resume behavior.
